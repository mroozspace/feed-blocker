# Privacy Policy – Feed Blocker

Feed Blocker does not collect, store, share or transmit any personal data.

- The extension runs entirely in your browser and contacts no servers.
- The only data saved is your on/off choice for each blocking rule, stored with the browser's `storage` API (and synced by your browser account if sync is enabled).
- It does not read or record the content of pages you visit, your browsing history, or your account details on YouTube, Instagram or Facebook.
- No analytics, advertising or tracking is used.
